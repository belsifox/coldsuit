Button guide

The external button and S2 on the board do the same thing. S3 switches Auto/Manual. Short press = under half a second, long press = hold it longer. Presses within 0.6 s of each other count as one sequence.

Manual any presspump on / off
Manual hold 5 sswitch to Auto (in Auto a 5 s hold does nothing)
Auto long pressforce pump ON for 5 min
Auto 2 short pressesforce pump OFF for 5 min
Auto 3 short pressescancel the hold
Auto 5+ short presseslockout (pump off until cleared here)

Repeating a hold restarts its timer. In lockout the buttons do nothing. The controller boots in Manual with the pump off.
